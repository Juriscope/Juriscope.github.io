About Juriscope

Juriscope was created to give all students the opportunity to display their works on topics they are passionate about. This space is for anyone of varying experiences, and we work closely with them along the path of publishing their work. No student should face inescapable barriers that block them from academic achievement.

Our story

Created by students, for students. We understand how difficult it can be to pursue an interest in law as a student. Especially when you feel like everyone else has access to mentors, established networks and official publication, and finding a place to start feels overwhelming. We created Juriscope to bridge this gap. Juriscope is built upon the beliefs that curiosity, thoughtful research, and meaningful ideas shouldn’t depend on the connections or opportunities someone already has. Our goal is to give students a platform to share their work, develop their perspectives, and experience publishing.

How review works

After you submit your work, our team will evaluate it based on its originality, strength of argument, use of evidence, organization and relevance to the subject. Submissions will then receive constructive feedback, if any, and be invited to make revisions before a final publication decision is made. Our goal is not simply to select strong writing, but to encourage students to develop their research and editing skills. Authors whose work is selected for publication receive a digital Certificate of Publication recognizing their contribution to Juriscope.

Privacy and policies

Chosen works are not required to be published with full names, as most of our submitters are minors. Instead, displaying first names, anonymous, or other options are allowed for the website. Submitting works that are plagiarized, written with AI, or without citing correct sources will not be published and you will not be allowed to submit any more. While AI detectors are not fully reliable, submitted writings will still be checked using them. If the work has an alarmingly high AI usage predictability percentage, that is where a problem arises. If you would like your work removed, contact our email address and it will be taken down.
